# Code of Conduct

This project is maintained by a small team and used by administrators who rely on it. We
want every issue, discussion and review here to stay useful and respectful.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Stay on topic: issues and discussions are about this MCP server and Access Server.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Do not post credentials, tokens, TOTP secrets or other people's private data, even
  "redacted" ones you are not sure about.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks in any form.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Deliberately derailing threads, spamming or trolling.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project happens: the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, code
review) and any public channel where you speak for the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers, using the same address as for
security reports (see `SECURITY.md`). Reports are handled confidentially; you will get an
acknowledgement within five working days.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content that breaks these rules and may warn, temporarily
block or permanently ban anyone who repeats or escalates. Decisions are explained to the
person concerned when it is safe to do so.

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), shortened to
what a small tool project needs.
